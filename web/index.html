Questions

Good to know

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. Kwik Budget never asks for your bank login. You log spending by typing it, and if you want, you can import a statement file you downloaded yourself.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start straight away. When you are ready, add an email in Settings to back up your data and use it on another phone.

Is it really free?

Yes. The daily number, jars, chat logging, bills and the weekly review are free forever, with no ads. Pro is optional.

Does it work in my currency?

Yes. Pick your currency in onboarding or Settings. Kwik understands local spending too, from jollof to bodaboda.
