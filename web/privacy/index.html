Privacy policy

This policy explains what Kwik Budget collects, why, who helps us run the app, and the choices you have. It applies to the Kwik Budget apps for iOS and Android and to this website.

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to work: your budget, bills and the entries you log.
  • No bank logins, no ads, no tracking across apps, and we never sell your data.
  • Kwik AI sends the text, statement or receipt you choose to Google Gemini to read it. It is not used to train AI models.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

Data you give us

  • Budget setup: your payday, pay amount, currency, savings goal, jars and their budgets.
  • Bills and subscriptions: names, amounts, due dates, billing period, trial end dates and whether they are paid.
  • Entries: amounts, merchants, categories, notes and dates for spending and extra income you log.
  • Merchant memory: how you file a merchant (for example “Mama's → Food”), so the app can do it for you next time.
  • Account details (optional): if you back up your data, your email address and a password. Passwords are stored by our database provider as a one-way hash; we cannot read them.
  • Support messages: what you send us by email.

Data created when you use the app

  • An account identifier. When you start, the app creates an anonymous account so your data can be saved securely. It is a random ID, not your name or phone number.
  • Kwik AI usage counts (how many AI requests you made today), to apply the Free and Pro limits.
  • Purchase status: whether you have Pro, your plan and its renewal or trial dates.

What we do not collect

  • Bank or card logins, card numbers or bank account numbers.
  • Your contacts, precise location, or advertising identifiers.
  • Analytics or advertising trackers. The app contains no ad SDKs.

Kwik AI, statements and receipts

Most entries are understood on your phone and on our servers by simple rules. Kwik AI is used only when you send something the rules cannot read, or when you choose to import a statement or scan a receipt.

  • When you use Kwik AI, the text you typed, the statement file (PDF or CSV) you picked, or the receipt photo you took is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and from there to Google's Gemini API, together with your currency, your category list and the names of your bills so it can be matched.
  • Gemini returns suggested entries. You review them and choose what to save. Only the entries you save are stored in your account.
  • We do not store statement files or receipt photos after they have been read.
  • We use Google's paid Gemini API service. Under Google's terms for that service, the content we send is not used to train or improve Google's models. Google may keep it for a limited time to detect abuse.
  • AI can make mistakes. Always check suggested entries before saving them.

Notifications

Reminders (daily check-in, bills, renewals, trial endings, payday) are scheduled on your phone by the app. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or your phone's settings at any time.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep data

  • Your budget data is kept while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, your account and all of its budget data are deleted from our live database straight away. Backups that may still contain it are overwritten within 30 days.
  • AI content is not stored by us after it has been read (see Kwik AI above).
  • Purchase records held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat are kept as their policies and tax law require.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Every row in our database is protected by access rules so that only your account can read or change it. AI keys and other secrets live only on our servers, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure, so if you back up with an email, use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: Pro users can export entries to CSV in the app. Anyone can ask us for a copy of their data.
  • Correct: edit or delete any entry, bill or jar in the app.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete account removes your account and data. See Delete your account.
  • Object or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where we rely on it.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, California, Brazil, Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya or South Africa), you may have some or all of these rights by law. We offer them to everyone. Email support@kwikbudget.app and we will reply within 30 days. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under California law.

Children

Kwik Budget is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age) and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
