Delete your Kwik Budget account

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time. Here is how, and exactly what happens.

On this page
  1. Delete from the app (fastest)
  2. If you no longer have the app
  3. What is deleted
  4. What may be kept
  5. Delete some data, keep your account

Delete from the app (fastest)

  1. Open Kwik Budget and go to the Today tab.
  2. Tap your profile picture at the top left to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted straight away and the app returns to the welcome screen.

Have Pro? Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions, or you may keep being charged. How to cancel.

If you no longer have the app

If you backed up with an email, you can either reinstall the app, sign in and delete from Settings, or ask us to do it:

  1. Email support@kwikbudget.app from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my account”.
  2. We may reply to confirm the request came from you.
  3. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and email you when it is done.

If you never backed up with an email, your data is linked only to the anonymous account inside the app on your phone. Open the app and use Settings → Delete account. If you have already removed the app, we have no way to link that data to you; email us if you need help.

What is deleted

  • Your account, including your email and password if you backed up.
  • Your budget profile, cycles, jars and their budgets.
  • Every entry, note, bill, subscription and extra income you logged.
  • Your merchant memory and your Kwik AI usage counts.

Data is removed from our live database immediately. Backups that may still hold it are overwritten within 30 days.

What may be kept

  • Purchase records. Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep records of Pro purchases as their policies and tax laws require. They hold a random account ID, not your budget data.
  • Support emails you sent us, for up to 2 years, unless you ask us to delete them too.

Delete some data, keep your account

You do not have to delete everything. In the app you can swipe to delete any entry, delete any bill or subscription, and remove jars. To delete only your email from a backed-up account, contact us.
