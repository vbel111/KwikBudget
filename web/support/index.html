Getting started

How is my daily number worked out?

Kwik Budget takes your pay (plus any extra income you log), sets aside every bill and subscription due before payday, and puts aside what you chose for savings. What is left is shared across the days until payday. Spend less today and tomorrow’s number grows; spend more and the remaining days adjust.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The app creates a private, anonymous account when you start so your data is saved. To keep your data if you change phones, open Settings and choose Back up your data to add an email and password.

Does Kwik Budget connect to my bank?

No, and it never asks for bank logins. You log spending by typing it in chat. With Kwik AI you can also import a statement file (PDF or CSV) you downloaded from your bank or mobile money app, or scan a receipt.

What can I type in chat?

Write it the way you would text it: lunch 8.50, uber 14 yesterday, coffee 4 and bread 6, rent paid, got 50 tips, dinner 60 split 3. Kwik learns how you file each place. If you change a category once, it remembers.

How do I change my currency or payday?

Currency is in Settings. Your payday and pay are set at the start of each cycle; when payday arrives, the app walks you through the next plan.

Kwik AI

What is Kwik AI?

Kwik AI helps when a message is too tricky for the built-in rules, and powers statement import and receipt scan. It suggests entries; you always check them before saving.

What are the limits?

Free: 3 AI messages a day, 1 statement import and 2 receipt scans to try. Pro: no daily cap, with fair use of 200 AI messages, 10 statements and 40 receipts a day.

Is my statement kept?

No. The file is read, you review the entries it finds, and only the ones you save are stored. Read more in the Privacy policy.

Pro and billing

How does the free trial work?

Yearly Pro starts with a 7-day free trial. You get every Pro feature straight away, the app reminds you before the trial ends, and you are charged only if you do not cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

How do I cancel?

iPhone: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Kwik Budget → Cancel subscription.
Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → Kwik Budget → Cancel.
You can also get there from Kwik Budget: Settings → Kwik Budget Pro. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds come from them. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open your order in Google Play and choose Request a refund. If you are having trouble, email us and we will help.

I bought Pro but the app does not show it

Open Settings → Restore purchases, making sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with. If that does not work, email us with the order ID from your receipt.

Your data

Is my data safe?

Your data travels encrypted and is locked so only your account can read it. We do not show ads, do not use trackers, and never sell your data. See the Privacy policy.

I got a new phone. How do I get my data back?

If you backed up with an email, install Kwik Budget, tap I already have an account on the welcome screen, and sign in. If you never backed up, the data was tied to the old install and cannot be moved.

Can I export my data?

Pro users can export entries to CSV from the Activity screen. Anyone can email us to ask for a copy of their data.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete account. Full details, including what to do if you no longer have the app, are on the Delete your account page.
