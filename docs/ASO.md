## 2. Description (iOS, not indexed, written for conversion)

Kwik Budget turns your paycheck into one number: what is safe to spend today.

Enter your payday and bills. Kwik Budget sets your bills aside first, fills your spending jars and divides what is left into a daily allowance. Spend less today and the extra rolls over. Spend more and tomorrow adjusts, so one bad day never breaks the month.

TYPE IT, DONE
Type "lunch 8.50" or "uber 14". Kwik picks the jar, shows what it does to your allowance and saves it in one tap.

MONEY JARS
Food, transport, fun and buffer drain as you spend. Bills and savings stay locked until you decide otherwise.

PAYDAY TIMELINE
Every bill between now and payday, and what stays safe to spend after each one.

WEEKLY REVIEW
Your streak of days under allowance, what rolled over, and a nudge to move spare money into savings.

No ads. Syncs securely across your devices. Kwik Budget is a budgeting and tracking tool, it does not move money or connect to your bank.

## 6. Launch plan
- Week 0: submit with promo text above. Ask for a rating only after a day finishes under allowance (the Review tab streak moment), never after an overspend. iOS limit is 3 prompts per year.
- Week 1 to 2: swap promo text for the seasonal hook. Use Custom Product Pages for ads: "Payday" (frame 3 first), "Student" (frame 2 first), "Couples" (later feature).
- In-app events: "Payday Reset" (name 30: "Payday Reset Challenge"; short 50: "Stay under allowance 7 days in a row"), recurring monthly. Black Friday and New Year "money reset" are the two biggest seasonal moments in the next 8 weeks, revert copy after each.
- Reply to every review in the first 60 days.
